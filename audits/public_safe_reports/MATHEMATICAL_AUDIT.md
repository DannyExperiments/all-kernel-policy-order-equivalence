# Mathematical audit

Full support gives strictly positive occupancies under a fully mixed policy. The baseline flow columns are invertible and their determinant has positive sign, so eliminating those columns preserves the orientation of each advantage numerator. The policy order comparison therefore includes the zero vector and every coordinate tie.

Each determinant uses every row group at most once. Restricting to a face preserves that property, and degrees within one row group add under polynomial multiplication. This proves that all nonconstant factors are rowwise affine and have multiplicity one. A mixed factor crosses zero along a segment between interior hybrids differing in one row, so a nonzero partial derivative gives its local zero as a rational graph. Divisibility follows by substituting this graph and applying primitivity. The other factors stay strictly signed on that relative interior.

The two product grids recover the degree-two polynomials by interpolation within each row. Boundary faces cover cases missed by a full-dimensional interior test. For the state–action companion the self-loop test fixes action differences, and an anchor with a nonzero action difference fixes the scale on redirects. If every action difference vanishes, the anchors compare state rewards directly.
