# Provenance

Catalog Q717 captures Joar Skalse and Alessandro Abate's all-transition-kernel question in Appendix A.7 of the public author version of *Partial Identifiability and Misspecification in Inverse Reinforcement Learning*. The same issue is posed in Skalse's Oxford dissertation. [Author version, p. 94](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.15951v1#page=94) · [Journal record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.artint.2026.104525) · [Pinned catalog entry](https://github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog/blob/ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe/problems/mathematics/optimization-operations-research.md#q717)

The handoff supplied the general criterion, an exact replay and a state–action supplement. The manuscript retains their full proofs and credits earlier fixed-kernel characterizations, published separation examples and general real-algebraic decidability.
